1. Information We Collect

SalaryFlow is designed to help users manage salary, expenses, budgets, savings, debts, and financial reports. Depending on how you use the app, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

Name, email address, login credentials, and profile details if you create an account.

Financial Information

Income entries, salary breakdowns, expenses, budgets, savings goals, debt information, and investment records entered by you.

Device & Technical Data

Device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, and diagnostics needed to maintain app performance and security.

Authentication Data

Biometric authentication status, encrypted tokens, and security preferences such as 2FA settings.

Some information may be stored locally on your device to support offline functionality. If cloud sync or account-based features are enabled, selected information may also be stored on our secure servers or trusted infrastructure providers.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to operate, improve, and secure SalaryFlow, including to:

  • Provide salary, expense, budgeting, savings, debt, and reporting features.
  • Sync your data across devices when online sync is enabled.
  • Send reminders and notifications for bills, budgets, and salary events.
  • Protect user accounts, detect fraud, and improve account security.
  • Analyze app performance, fix bugs, and improve user experience.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
We do not sell your personal financial data. We use your information only for legitimate business, operational, security, and product-improvement purposes described in this policy.

3. Data Sharing

We may share limited information with trusted service providers only when necessary to operate the app, such as hosting providers, analytics providers, authentication services, notification services, or crash reporting tools.

  • To provide infrastructure, cloud storage, and secure account services.
  • To send push notifications and reminders.
  • To monitor stability, detect abuse, and improve reliability.
  • To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or government requests.

We do not share your personal data with advertisers for sale or unrelated marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

  • Encrypted transmission using secure protocols such as HTTPS/TLS.
  • Secure storage practices for credentials and authentication tokens.
  • Access controls and limited administrative access to production systems.
  • Optional biometric protection on supported devices.
  • Monitoring and updates to address app security and service stability.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Data stored locally on your device remains under your control unless synchronized to a cloud account. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, unless longer retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Request a copy of your data, where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent for optional features, such as marketing or certain notifications.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us at support@salaryflow.app.

7. Children's Privacy

SalaryFlow is not directed to children under the age of 13, or the minimum age required in your country to use digital services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. International Use

If you use SalaryFlow from outside the country where our servers or service providers are located, your information may be transferred to and processed in other jurisdictions. We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers where required by law.

9. Third-Party Services

SalaryFlow may rely on third-party tools and infrastructure, such as cloud hosting, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, or push notification services. These providers may process limited data on our behalf according to their own privacy terms and contractual obligations.

If bank integrations, payment integrations, or financial data import services are enabled in future versions, additional disclosures may be added to this Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements, or data handling practices. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and may provide additional notice inside the app or through other appropriate channels.
