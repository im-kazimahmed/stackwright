SafeNest Privacy Policy

SafeNest is a family safety application that provides live location sharing, family circles, safe-zone alerts, SOS emergency notifications, and device monitoring features. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage your personal data for Google Play and Apple App Store compliance.

Effective Date: April 19, 2026
Contact: support@safenest.app

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use SafeNest:

  • Name and profile details
  • Email address and login credentials
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Profile photo (if uploaded)
  • Precise and approximate location data
  • Battery status and GPS/device status
  • Device identifiers and push notification tokens
  • App interactions, diagnostics, and crash logs

2. Location Data

SafeNest uses foreground and background location access to provide continuous family safety features. This includes live family location sharing, safe-zone enter/exit alerts, offline detection, and SOS emergency support.

Your location is only shared with approved members inside your private family circle. SafeNest does not sell your location data.

Background location is used only after your explicit consent and while location sharing is enabled. A persistent system notification is shown while background tracking is active.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide live family location sharing
  • Send safe-zone enter/exit alerts
  • Deliver SOS emergency alerts
  • Monitor battery and device connectivity
  • Improve app reliability and prevent abuse
  • Provide customer support and account security

4. Data Sharing

We may share limited information only when necessary:

  • With approved family circle members for live location visibility
  • With trusted service providers such as Firebase, hosting, analytics, and push notification services
  • When required by law or legal process

We do not sell personal data to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

5. Data Security

  • HTTPS/TLS encrypted communication
  • Secure authentication and token storage
  • Access controls and protected backend infrastructure
  • Biometric authentication where supported
  • Monitoring and security updates

6. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service, ensure family safety, comply with legal obligations, and resolve support requests.

Location history and account data may be deleted when you remove your account or request deletion.

7. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and personal data
  • Disable location sharing at any time
  • Withdraw permission for notifications or optional permissions

8. Children & Families

SafeNest is designed for family safety and may be used by parents and guardians to manage family location sharing. The app is not intended for unsupervised use by children under 13. Parents or legal guardians should manage child accounts where applicable.

9. Third-Party Services

SafeNest may use trusted third-party services including:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications)
  • Firebase Analytics / Crash Reporting
  • Google Maps
  • Secure cloud hosting infrastructure

10. Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion directly inside the app through Settings → Delete Account, or through our web deletion request page.

Delete Account URL: https://safenest.kazimahmed.com/account/delete

Once approved, personal data including family circles, saved locations, and account details will be deleted within a reasonable time unless retention is required by law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and may also be communicated inside the app.
